About Me

I am motivated to create work that is distinctive, thoughtful, and boundary-pushing. I believe that risk-taking is an essential part of growth as a designer. Rather than defaulting to what feels safe or familiar, I intentionally experiment with unconventional compositions, unexpected materials, and bold conceptual directions. Standing out, to me, is not about being different for the sake of it, but about discovering new visual languages and perspectives that feel authentic and meaningful.
